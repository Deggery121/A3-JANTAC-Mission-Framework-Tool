    Installing by hand
    
      
      
    1. Unzip the download.
      2. 
      
    2. Copy the folders inside compositions to your Arma profile's compositions folder: Documents\Arma 3 - Other Profiles\<profile>\compositions (or Documents\Arma 3\compositions). If your Documents folder lives in OneDrive, look under OneDrive\Documents.
      3. 
      
    3. Copy the loadout file to your mission's customization\loadouts folder and add the gear.sqf line inside switch (_type) do { … }. A ready-made gear.sqf is in the zip if your mission has no custom lines.
      4. 
      
    4. In Eden, open Compositions → JANTAC Mission Framework and place your package.
      5. 
      
    5. Set maxPlayers in customization\missionSettings.hpp to the slot count shown in the manifest.
      6.
