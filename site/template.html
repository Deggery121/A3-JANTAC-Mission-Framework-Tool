    Installing by hand
    
      
      
    1. Unzip the download.
      2. 
      
    2. Copy the folders inside compositions to your Arma profile's compositions folder: Documents\Arma 3 - Other Profiles\<profile>\compositions (or Documents\Arma 3\compositions). If your Documents folder lives in OneDrive, look under OneDrive\Documents.
      3. 
      
    3. Copy the loadout files to your mission's customization\loadouts folder and add their gear.sqf lines inside switch (_type) do { … }. A ready-made gear.sqf is in the zip if your mission has no custom lines.
      4. 
      
    4. In Eden, open Compositions → JANTAC Mission Framework and place your units.
      5. 
      
    5. If you wrote a briefing, copy customization\briefing.sqf over your mission's.
      6. 
      
    6. Copy customization\missionSettings.hpp over your mission's, or set maxPlayers in yours to the number shown in the manifest (it counts the headless client).
      7.
