    How this works
    
      This page sets up the player side of a JANTAC (Olsen-based) mission: the playable units with their gear, the briefing and the mission settings. It doesn't make objectives. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded, and your drafts are kept in this browser until you clear them.
      

    
      1. Pick your units (Units tab)
      
    
        
      
      • BLUFOR, OPFOR and INDFOR list the ready-made platoon packages for each side. Pick one and choose how many squads go under 1'0. Every slot already has its gear script, team color, lobby name and ACRE radio channel.
        • 
      
      • Custom options has two more choices. Every custom platoon you've saved in the Custom units tab is listed there, with Edit and Delete. My mission already has units is for missions that already have their loadout and units placed: you get only the briefing and mission settings.
        • 
      
      • Click a selected card again to unselect it.
        • 
    
      

    
      2. Build your own platoon (Custom units tab, optional)
      
    
        
      
      • Use New design to start empty or from a package, and choose a side. Pick a group on the right and click units on the left to add them. You can reorder, rename, set red or blue teams, duplicate squads and add squads.
        • 
      
      • Press Save to keep the design. Saved designs are listed at the top of the tab and under Custom options on the Units tab, where you can edit or delete them. They're kept in this browser.
        • 
      
      • The first slot in each group is its leader. Medics are always green, and SL and PL roles stay on the default team.
        • 
      
      • You can mix units from different packages of the same side. Each unit keeps its own gear script, and every loadout the design uses is added.
        • 
      
      • Press Save and use this design to save it and select it for the mission.
        • 
    
      

    
      3. Write the briefing (Briefing tab, optional)
      
    
      First choose COOP or TVT. COOP is one briefing for your side. TVT gives each team its own briefing: pick team 2's side, then switch between Team 1 and Team 2 to write each one. The mission title, mission notes and CO-only tab are shared. Picking one also sets the COOP/TVT prefix in the Mission folder tab, and the other way round.
      
    
      Fill in the sections you need and leave the rest empty. Empty sections are left out. Friendly composition and the radio list fill in from your units. The CO-only tab is shown only to the platoon commander's unit. The preview shows the exact briefing.sqf you'll get.
      

    
      4. Set the mission settings (Mission settings tab, optional)
      
    
      Loading screen name and messages, an optional loading image, game type and player limits. maxPlayers fills in from your units: player slots plus the headless client.
      

    
      5. Name the mission folder (Mission folder tab, optional)
      
    
      Pick COOP or TVT and the player slots for the prefix (the slots fill in from your units), type a mission name and pick the map. Arma needs the folder to end in the map's world name, for example MyMission.VTF_Korsac_Winter for Korsac (Winter); the page builds that for you. Tick Put it in a mission folder and the download puts everything inside that folder, and Create mission folder… (Chrome or Edge) makes it in your profile's missions folder. While it's ticked, Add to a mission… is turned off.
      

    
      TVT: Team 2 and playing a platoon as another side
      
    
      Open a package and use Play as to place it as BLUFOR, OPFOR or INDFOR. The gear and roles stay the same; the units become that side's classes, so faces and voices follow the new side. For a TVT mission, tick Add a Team 2 (TVT) under the list: pick the other platoon, its side and squad count. Both compositions and both loadouts go into the mission, maxPlayers counts both teams, and the briefing switches to TVT with Team 2's side, composition and radios filled in. If either side is INDFOR, set who INDFOR is enemy to in Eden (Attributes > General); INSTALL.txt reminds you.
      
    
      6. Take it to your mission (manifest on the Units tab)
      
    
        
      
      • Get the framework adds the whole JANTAC framework (pick the version with modules or without when there's more than one), so you get a complete mission folder without copying the framework yourself. The framework needs four files the page makes for you: gear.sqf and a loadout (from your units), briefing.sqf (Briefing tab) and missionSettings.hpp (Mission settings tab). While it's ticked, the briefing and mission settings are always included, and the download stays off until the Units, Briefing and Mission settings tabs are filled in; the list under the checkbox shows what's left.
        • 
      
      • Tick Include briefing.sqf and Include missionSettings.hpp to add them. They tick themselves once you start editing.
        • 
      
      • Download zip works in any browser. It holds the compositions, the loadout files, a gear.sqf, your briefing and settings, and an INSTALL.txt.
        • 
      
      • Add to a mission… (Chrome or Edge, on the GitHub page) writes into a mission folder you pick. It copies the loadouts, adds their lines to gear.sqf without touching your other lines, and replaces the briefing and settings. Any file it replaces is kept with .bak added to its name.
        • 
      
      • Install compositions… (not required) copies the compositions straight into your profile's compositions folder. You can copy them from the zip instead.
        • 
    
      

    
      7. Place the units in Eden
      
    
      Open Compositions → JANTAC Mission Framework and place your package or custom platoon. Squad 1'N is on PRC-343 channel N and 1'0 is on the channel after the last squad. Then set the mission up as usual.
      
  
      

  
      
    Installing by hand
    
        
      
      1. Unzip the download.
        2. 
      
      2. Copy the folders inside compositions to your Arma profile's compositions folder: Documents\Arma 3 - Other Profiles\<profile>\compositions (or Documents\Arma 3\compositions). If your Documents folder lives in OneDrive, look under OneDrive\Documents.
        3. 
      
      3. Copy the loadout files to your mission's customization\loadouts folder and add their gear.sqf lines inside switch (_type) do { … }. A ready-made gear.sqf is in the zip if your mission has no custom lines.
        4. 
      
      4. In Eden, open Compositions → JANTAC Mission Framework and place your units.
        5. 
      
      5. If you wrote a briefing, copy customization\briefing.sqf over your mission's.
        6. 
      
      6. If you ticked Get the framework, the mission folder in the zip is already complete: copy it into your profile's missions folder.
        7. 
      
      7. Copy customization\missionSettings.hpp over your mission's, or set maxPlayers in yours to the number shown in the manifest (it counts the headless client).
        8.

          Mission folder
          
          
            
          
              
            
          
            
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
        
          
        
          Arma ties a mission to its map by the part after the dot: a mission on Korsac (Winter) lives in a folder called MissionName.VTF_Korsac_Winter. If the world name doesn't match the map, Arma won't show the mission on that map.
          
        
          The prefix shows the game type and player slots, like CO30_ or TVT40_. Mission names use letters, numbers, _ and -. Spaces become _, and anything else is removed.
